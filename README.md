# HR Employee Attrition Analysis 📊

## Overview

This project performs an **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on an HR Employee Attrition dataset to uncover the key factors influencing employee turnover. The analysis focuses on identifying patterns, trends, and business insights that can help organizations improve employee retention and reduce attrition-related costs.

## Business Problem

Employee attrition can lead to:

* Increased hiring and training costs
* Loss of organizational knowledge
* Project delays and reduced productivity
* Team instability and lower employee morale

The objective of this analysis is to understand:

* Why employees leave the organization
* Which employee groups are most likely to leave
* The factors that contribute most to attrition
* Data-driven strategies to improve retention

## Project Workflow

1. **Business Understanding**
2. **Data Loading & Inspection**
3. **Data Quality Checks**

   * Missing value analysis
   * Outlier detection
4. **Data Preprocessing**
5. **Univariate Analysis**
6. **Bivariate & Multivariate Analysis**
7. **Statistical Analysis & Hypothesis Testing**
8. **Visualization & Insight Generation**

## Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib

* Scikit-learn

## Key Analysis Areas

* Employee demographics
* Job satisfaction and work-life balance
* Income and compensation trends
* Overtime impact on attrition
* Job roles and departmental attrition
* Performance and career growth factors

## Outcomes

The project provides actionable insights into employee behavior and identifies the most significant drivers of attrition, helping HR teams make informed decisions to improve employee engagement and retention.
